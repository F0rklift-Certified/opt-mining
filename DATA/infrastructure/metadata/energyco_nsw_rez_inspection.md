# NSW EnergyCo REZ Boundary Inspection

EnergyCo publishes state-level GIS boundary downloads from its official REZ
pages. I downloaded three Shapefile ZIP packages on 2026-08-18:

| Package | Contents | CRS | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| `energyco_new_england_rez_boundary.zip` | New England boundary Shapefile components | GDA94 geographic (EPSG:4283 convention) | NSW REZ screening |
| `energyco_central_west_orana_rez_boundary.zip` | Central-West Orana boundary Shapefile components | GDA94 geographic (EPSG:4283 convention) | NSW REZ screening |
| `energyco_hunter_central_coast_rez_boundary.zip` | Hunter-Central Coast boundary Shapefile components | GDA94 geographic (EPSG:4283 convention) | NSW REZ screening |

The official EnergyCo pages describe these as geographical-area boundary GIS
files. They are the appropriate state-level source for NSW REZ overlays. Keep
their source page, download date and GDA94 CRS when converting them for model
ingestion. They do not establish spare network capacity or a project approval.
